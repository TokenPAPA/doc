## 7 Cost-Saving Tricks That Keep You Under $10

1. **Set `max_tokens` everywhere** — one runaway response can cost 20x a normal one.
2. **Cache repeated calls** — DeepSeek's context cache makes repeat inputs ~90% cheaper.
3. **Use Flash for everything first** — only route to premium models when users actually pay.
4. **Shorten system prompts** — every 100 tokens you trim saves across thousands of requests.
5. **Batch what you can** — combine small tasks into one prompt instead of many calls.
6. **Stream responses** — better UX *and* lower perceived cost; users cancel early.
7. **Log usage daily** — a 5-line script tells you if you're on track or bleeding.
