# How to Top Up TokenPAPA: Stripe & Waffo Pancake Payment Guide

Topping up your TokenPAPA account just got easier. TokenPAPA now supports **two payment channels — Stripe and Waffo Pancake** — covering **WeChat Pay, Alipay, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank cards**. Minimum top-up is only **$10**, and your balance is credited **instantly** after payment.

This guide covers every payment method, the step-by-step top-up process, and answers to common questions.

## How to Top Up (Step by Step)

1. **Log in** to [tokenpapa.ai](https://tokenpapa.ai) and go to the Top Up page: [https://tokenpapa.ai/console/topup](https://tokenpapa.ai/console/topup)

2. **Enter an amount** — select a preset amount or type a custom one (minimum **$10**)

3. **Choose a payment channel** — Stripe or Waffo Pancake

4. **Pick your payment method** — WeChat Pay, Alipay, bank card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay (depending on the channel)

5. **Click "Top Up"** — you'll be redirected to the payment platform

6. **Complete payment** — you'll be redirected back to TokenPAPA automatically

7. **Done!** — your balance updates instantly and you can start calling APIs right away

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pay with WeChat Pay?
Yes — WeChat Pay is supported through **both Stripe and Waffo Pancake** channels.

### What is the minimum top-up?
The minimum top-up is **$10 USD**.

### How long until my balance updates?
Instantly. After payment completes you're redirected back automatically and your balance is credited immediately.

### Is there a fee for topping up?
Payment processing fees (if any) are shown on the payment page before you confirm. The amount you enter is the amount credited to your balance.

### What if my payment fails?
Check that your card is enabled for international/online payments, or try the other payment channel (Stripe ↔ Waffo Pancake). If the problem persists, contact [support@tokenpapa.ai](mailto:support@tokenpapa.ai) or join our [Telegram](https://t.me/+fI2mYwHoZN5lYmVl).
